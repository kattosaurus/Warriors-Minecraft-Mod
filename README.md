Live the world of **Warriors** by Erin Hunter inside Minecraft. Play as a cat, choose your Clan, shape your look, and experience the story through cutscenes and two very different game modes.

> **Status:** This mod is a work in progress. Features may change, and some listed below are still planned.

---

## Features

### Game modes

You pick one game mode when you begin. Each one has its own gameplay, mechanics and style, so choose wisely: you only get one choice per world.

#### Vanilla
Play the normal Minecraft experience, but as a cat.

- Regular Minecraft world, including hostile mobs like zombies
- Mine at normal speed
- Go and beat the Ender Dragon
- Keep your cat form and traits throughout

#### Realistic
Live the life of a warrior. This is a Hardcore, RPG-style experience in a custom dimension.

- Be born, get a mentor, and complete patrols (quests)
- Become a warrior, and maybe one day a deputy or leader
- Hostile and unrealistic mobs do not spawn
- Mining is heavily limited, so the focus is on story and roleplay
- You live until you die. If you die, the world is over and you must start a new one
- Skip patrols and break the warrior code, or follow it and join StarClan after death

### Cutscenes

- **Spawn / Rebirth:** choose your game mode, appearance and more
- **Apprentice ceremony:** just like in the books
- **Warrior ceremony:** just like in the books
- **Deputy ceremony:** just like in the books
- **Leader ceremony:** a reimagined version of the moonstone ceremony, where you receive your nine lives
- **Post-death:** wake up in the afterlife you earned, either StarClan (good ending) or the Dark Forest, with special interactions in each

### Clans

Make the Clans your own.

- Choose how many Clans exist
- Choose the terrain each Clan's territory generates on
- Name your Clans
- Further customization options

### Customization

Make your cat truly yours.

- Tinting
- Simple premade coat patterns
- Eye colors
- Accessories

**Planned:** a deeper pattern system, similar to *Warrior Cats: Ultimate Edition* on Roblox, and other extras.

## Requirements

| Requirement | Version |
|-------------|---------|
| Minecraft   | 1.20.1  |
| Forge       | 47.x.x  |
| Java        | 17      |

**Dependencies:** none. No other mods are required.

## Installation

1. Install Forge for the Minecraft version above.
2. Download the latest `.jar` from CurseForge, Modrinth or the GitHub releases page.
3. Put the `.jar` in your `mods` folder.
4. Launch the game and create a new world.
